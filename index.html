Before the laser: a short studio checklist +

New recipes use closed red cutouts, not score-line drawings. Assign red to cut in Glowforge and verify imported dimensions. Blue only appears in saved first-draft score studies.

Use only plywood approved for your laser. Unknown glues, coatings, or treatments may be unsafe. Consult the machine and material guidance, use approved ventilation, and never leave a running laser unattended.

Reserved wood is a geometric spacing target before kerf, not a strength rating. Small openings are omitted, but sharp tips can remain. The app does not model kerf, burning, or fit. Inspect paths and test a small scrap of your actual material.

The wood preview is an illustration, not a fabrication simulation. All exports include a rectangular boundary cut; disable it if you only want interior details on an existing panel. New recipes intend a single connected panel, not a layered 3D object.

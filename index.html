Before the laser: a short studio checklist +

New recipes use closed red cutouts, not score-line drawings. Assign red to cut in Glowforge and verify imported dimensions. Blue only appears in saved first-draft score studies.

Use only plywood approved for your laser. Unknown glues, coatings, or treatments may be unsafe. Consult the machine and material guidance, use approved ventilation, and never leave a running laser unattended.

Cell spacing and tracery spoke width are independent geometric targets before kerf, not strength ratings. Small openings are omitted, but sharp tips can remain. Inspect paths and test a small scrap of your actual material.

Two woods is an illustrative backing preview, not an inlay or fit calculation. Cutting one species and mounting the connected panel over another is an option; loose cutouts need separate handling. All exports include a rectangular boundary cut. Each four-panel file is one panel, not a combined sheet.
